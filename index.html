IF THIS IS YOUR FIRST TIME

New to AA?

Alcoholics Anonymous is a fellowship of people who share their experience, strength, and hope with each other so they can solve their common problem and help others recover from alcoholism. The only requirement for membership is a desire to stop drinking.

There are no dues or fees. Meetings are run entirely by members — there's no professional staff, and nothing is expected of you beyond showing up.


  • Open meetings
    Anyone is welcome — family, friends, or anyone curious about AA.

    Closed meetings
    For those who have a drinking problem, or think they might.

    Anonymity
    Who you see and what you hear stays in the room.
